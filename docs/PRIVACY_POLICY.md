# Privacy Policy for Vantage 419

**Last Updated:** February 11, 2026

**Vantage 419** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We primarily built Vantage 419 as a local-first utility.
*   **We do NOT collect personal data**: We do not require account creation, email addresses, or phone numbers.
*   **We do NOT track you**: We do not use analytics services (like Firebase or Google Analytics) to track your usage behavior or location history on our servers.

## 2. Information Stored Locally
The App stores specific data strictly on your device to function:
*   **Visit History**: A list of spot IDs you have visited is stored locally to power the weighted recommendation algorithm. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by clearing App Data.
*   **Preferences**: Your chosen Theme Mode (Light/Dark) and "First Launch" flags are stored in local SharedPreferences.

## 3. Location Data
*   **Usage**: The App requests access to your device's location to center the map on your position and show your distance to spots.
*   **Storage**: Your precise location is **processed ephemerally** on your device. It is NOT transmitted to our servers or stored permanently.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App interacts with the following third-party services:
*   **OpenStreetMap (via flutter_map)**: Used to display map tiles. Your IP address may be visible to map tile providers during requests.
*   **Google Maps / Waze / Apple Maps**: When you click "Navigate", the App launches your installed navigation app. We pass the destination coordinates to these apps; their respective privacy policies apply thereafter.
