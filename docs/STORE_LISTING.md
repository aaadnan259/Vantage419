## Full Description

**Discover Toledo, One Spin at a Time.** 🎰

Stuck in a rut going to the same three restaurants? Vantage 419 gamifies local discovery in Toledo, Ohio. Whether you're hungry, feeling active, or just want a surprise, spin the roulette to find your next adventure.

**Features:**
*   **The Roulette**: A weighted random selection engine that learns what you like.
*   **Smart Discovery**: Spots you haven't visited recently get higher priority (3x weight).
*   **Mood Modes**: Filter by "Hungry" (Dining/Café), "Active" (Fitness/Rec), or "Surprise Me".
*   **OLED Dark Mode**: A stunning, battery-saving dark interface perfect for night explorations.
*   **Light Mode**: Crisp, clean theme for daylight use.
*   **Privacy First**: Your location data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking.

**Why Vantage 419?**
We built this because Toledo has amazing spots that deserve more love. Stop asking "Where should we go?" and let the wheel decide.

Download now and rediscover the 419!
